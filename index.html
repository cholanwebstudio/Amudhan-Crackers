Amudhan Crackers

Where Every
Celebration Begins

Amudhan Crackers brings together a curated range of fireworks and crackers for Diwali and every festive occasion — from the first flicker of a sparkler to the finale of an aerial shell. We put together our collection with one idea in mind: a celebration that feels as bright as the people sharing it.

Since 2015, our 2-acre showroom in Nochikadu, Cuddalore has been a welcoming place for families, retailers and event organisers across Tamil Nadu to shop for crackers in person — with 200+ new fancy cracker varieties, sales 365 days a year and a 17,000 sq ft car park.
