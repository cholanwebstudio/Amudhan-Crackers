Amudhan Crackers

Where Every
Celebration Begins

Amudhan Crackers brings together a curated range of fireworks and crackers for Diwali and every festive occasion — from the first flicker of a sparkler to the finale of an aerial shell. We put together our collection with one idea in mind: a celebration that feels as bright as the people sharing it.

Our showroom in Nochikadu, Thiyagavalli is a straightforward, welcoming place for families, retailers and event organisers across Tamil Nadu to shop for crackers in person, ask questions, and choose exactly what their celebration needs.
